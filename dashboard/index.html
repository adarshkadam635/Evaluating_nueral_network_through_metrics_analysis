A reproducible pipeline

No test-driven model selection

  1. Download & verifyImmutable upstream commit; six SHA-256 checksums.
  2. Clean split overlaps33 training examples removed. Validation and test counts unchanged.
  3. Learn from training only15,000 tokens · sequence length 64 · trainable 64-D embeddings.
  4. Stop on validation lossAdam · dropout 0.4 · batch size 256 · patience 2 · up to 8 epochs.
  5. Choose, then reportValidation macro F1 selects the model. Report all held-out test metrics.

Read results with context

These small networks are trained from scratch on historical English tweets. Sarcasm, domain shift, class imbalance, and simple punctuation normalization limit their performance. Emoji and hashtag information may be lost.

Metrics reflect one seeded run, without confidence intervals. The cleaned splits and three-class labels differ from the original notebook; historical accuracies are not directly comparable.

TweetEval: Barbieri et al. (2020). Sentiment task: Rosenthal, Farra & Nakov (2017). The upstream card lists the sentiment subset under CC BY 3.0.
